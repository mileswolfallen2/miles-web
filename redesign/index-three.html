About

I'm Miles Wolf Allen, a developer & 3D artist still in school — and currently co-founder of Diskette Labs.

Right now I'm building Gelectron, a drop-in Electron replacement that uses native web views instead of bundling an entire Chromium. I've also built FEDL, OmniEmu, CommandMove, and 160+ other repos.

I believe pancakes are better than waffles, cereal is soup, and software should feel good to use.
