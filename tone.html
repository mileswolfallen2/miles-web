The honesty block

What's real

Binaural beats genuinely entrain your brainwaves — but the response varies person to person, and they need headphones. Noise masking (brown / pink / white) has real research behind it for focus and is pure, non-magical sound-blocking. Paced breathing tones (slow, rise-and-fall) are the one audio intervention with solid evidence — they're just giving you a rhythm to breathe to.

What's vibes

The "healing frequencies" (396, 432, 528, 639, 741, 852, 963 Hz) come from 1970s solfeggio spirituality, not research. The claims about DNA repair and fear-release have no peer-reviewed support. They're here because they're popular — if a slow tone relaxes you, that's your nervous system, and that's still a real win.
